# toriymi // Project8
> cursed apple — find your way through the city

My first website, created in 12 hours.
The site has two themes that you can switch between.

[To day] Dark Amber
A deep black-brown background with a soft golden-amber glow. Accents are in rich gold shades, and the text is a warm cream. It creates a cozy, elegant, and premium atmosphere.
[To night] Midnight Violet
A deep black background with a soft blue-violet glow. Accents are in lavender and cool violet shades, and the text is a light gray-lavender. It creates a calm, mysterious, and futuristic atmosphere.
